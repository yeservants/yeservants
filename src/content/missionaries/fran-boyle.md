  <b>About</b>
  God developed in me a heart for the Nations through International student ministry beginning in 1984. I was mentored by YES Missionary Leiton Chinn during the years of International Student Ministry. In 1992 I began ministry trips to South and Central Africa. In the mid 90s I began to feel a burden for Southern Sudan. In 1999, while Sudan was in the middle of war, I was invited to be part of a Team to the South.
  
  <b>Ministry</b>
  I have been traveling back to Southern Sudan every year since 1999, supporting the indigenous church through teaching and by raising support for medical help, care for orphans, scholarships for pastors, and other practical support.
  
  <b>Personal Message</b>
  I am grateful for the faithful financial support of the many friends who have stood with me and believed in and encouraged me over the years.
