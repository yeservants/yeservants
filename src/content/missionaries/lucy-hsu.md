  <b>About</b>
  Lucy Hsu has been involved with cross-cultural ministry since 2000. She volunteered then became a staff member at the International Christian Church of San Diego in 2003. In 2006, Lucy moved to Madison, Wisconsin serving as the National Administrator for the International Student Ministry at InterVarsity Christian Fellowship. Lucy has a Master of Arts in Theology from Fuller Theological Seminary.
  
  <b>Ministry</b>
  Lucy's current ministry focuses on international students and scholars. She partners with InterVarsity's International Student Chapter at the University of Texas in Austin. Lucy also has a heart for immigrants, refugees and the urban poor.
