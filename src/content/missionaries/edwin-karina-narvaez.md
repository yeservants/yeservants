  <b>About</b>
  Edwin and Karina Narvaez, born in Nicaragua and Mexico respectively, members of Greeneway Church in Orlando FL. In 2011 Edwin felt the call to be a missionary to Nicaragua, his ministry consists of continually traveling to different regions of that country, bringing Christ's voice of hope, trying to touch the most needy, those who live in garbage dumps and very poor regions, bringing them aid and food.
  
  Both have felt from the Lord Jesus Christ to dedicate themselves full time in the Kingdom of God. Their main mission is to carry the message of salvation and hope, help children in need by providing them with food, clothing, shoes, backpacks with school supplies and toys at Christmas time.
  
  Please, keep them in prayer so that they can achieve the plans that God has placed in their hearts.
