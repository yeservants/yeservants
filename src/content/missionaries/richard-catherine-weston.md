  <b>About</b>
  Since 1985 Richard and Catherine Weston have been involved with ministry among international students (ISM) in a variety of roles. In local ministry they have served in both Cambridge and Oxford. Richard has also served as National Director (CEO) of Friends International 1999-2008. Their vision and gifting has been to pioneer and nurture ISM in new places both in the UK and internationally.
  
  <b>Ministry</b>
  International students continue to feature in our lives. Catherine is involved in the local Friends International team, supporting and mentoring the next generation alongside writing resources. Richard is focusing on helping our Oxford church think through how to be more welcoming to ethnic minorities.
  
  <b>Personal Message</b>
  We continue to need to raise 100% of our support and we are grateful for the opportunity, via Y.E.S. Ministries, to have a channel for tax efficient giving from US sources.
