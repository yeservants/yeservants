Name:              Yielded Evangelical Servants (YES)
Type:              Marketing / Nonprofit Site — Real client site
Source of truth:   YES_Website_Copy_V3.pdf (34-page client copy & structure guide)
Purpose:           Convince serious donors, church partners, and supporters
                   that YES is a trustworthy, Gospel-centered, field-proven
                   ministry worthy of long-term partnership. Drive monthly
                   partnership and church/mission-team engagement.

Positioning:       A missionary-FOUNDED strengthening ministry. Not a sending
                   agency — YES strengthens faithful workers already in the
                   field. Field-born (founders served the Yanomàmö of
                   Venezuela). 35+ years. Authority from experience;
                   conviction from Scripture; stories from the field.

Tagline:           Strengthening Gospel Workers. Sustaining Kingdom Impact.
Alt taglines:      "Faithful workers. Faithful support. For the long haul."
                   "Advancing the Gospel through those already serving."
Core thematic:     "If we are one Body, no servant of Christ should stand alone."
                   (verbatim, used with weight — Hero + Biblical Foundation only)
Theology:          John 17:21 · John 13:35 · Romans 10:14–15 · Romans 10:15

Aesthetic Tone:    Reverent Editorial Authority. Premium faith-magazine
                   gravity. Deep forest-green dominance, generous whitespace,
                   large editorial serif. Dignity always — real field
                   photography, never poverty spectacle, never stock.
                   "Let it breathe. Let impact lines sit in silence."

Heading Font:      Playfair Display (large editorial serif; kinetic SplitText
                   mask-up reveals on hero + section headlines)
Body Font:         Lora (warm readable serif; Source Serif 4 is the alt)
                   Body min 17–18px · line-height ≥ 1.7
