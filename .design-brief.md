  3. Real photos only, dignity always.

  5. CTAs orange/gold; full-width on mobile; never buried under long text.
  6. Give form: monthly default, minimal steps, mobile-first.
  7. Scriptures: 4 cards on home; John 17:21 ends Give; John 13:35 ends Contact.
